I am a computational scientist and applied mathematician based in Ottawa, Canada. I develop, analyze, and implement numerical methods for partial differential equations, with an emphasis on structure preservation, efficiency, and scalability. I currently hold a postdoctoral fellowship in Raymond Spiteri's group at the University of Saskatchewan, supported by an NSERC Canada Postdoctoral Research Award. I'm also the founder of <a href ="https://nonlinearnumerics.com">Nonlinear Numerics Inc.</a>, an active open-source contributor, and a member of the <a href="https://github.com/trixi-framework/Trixi.jl">Trixi.jl</a> development team. I previously worked as a postdoc with <a href="https://www.mi.uni-koeln.de/NumSim/gregor-gassner/">Gregor Gassner</a> at the University of Cologne and completed my PhD in 2024 at the University of Toronto under the supervision of <a href="http://goldfinger.utias.utoronto.ca/dwz/">David Zingg</a>.
